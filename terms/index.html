What the service is, and what it is not

Spoke Savvy keeps a record of your bikes, parts, tools, and service history, adds distance from rides you import from Apple Health, estimates when parts are due for service, and offers a chat assistant that answers bike-maintenance questions using your bike's details and the documents you add.

It is a record-keeping and information tool, not a mechanic. In particular:

  • Service-due estimates come from the distances and intervals recorded in the app. They do not see the actual condition of your bike. A part can wear out sooner than the app says, for example after a crash, in wet or muddy conditions, or if a ride was never imported. Inspect your bike yourself.
  • Chat answers are generated by an AI model and can be wrong, including torque values, part compatibility, procedures, and quotes from documents. Treat them as a starting point, check them against the manufacturer's documentation, and stop and ask a qualified mechanic if you are unsure. Do not rely on the chat for anything where a mistake could cause injury.
  • Part suggestions and shopping links are suggestions only. Check that a part fits your bike before you buy it. Purchases happen on the retailer's site under the retailer's terms; we are not a party to them.
  • Health data is used only to add distance to your bikes. Spoke Savvy is not a medical or fitness device and gives no health advice.
